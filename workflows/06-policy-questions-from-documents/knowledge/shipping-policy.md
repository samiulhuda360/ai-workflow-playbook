# Shipping policy (customer-facing, version 4, August 2026)

Questions about this policy: ask the Operations lead.

## §1 New Zealand delivery

Orders placed before 1 pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. Delivery takes 1 to 3 working days in
the North Island and 2 to 4 working days in the South Island. Rural addresses can take 2 extra days.

## §2 Shipping costs in New Zealand

Shipping is $7.50 per order, and free on orders of $80 or more.

## §3 Australia

We ship to Australia from our Sydney warehouse. Delivery takes 3 to 7 working days. Shipping is A$12, free on
orders of A$120 or more.

## §4 Lost or damaged parcels

If tracking hasn't changed for 5 working days, we open a claim with the courier and send a replacement once the
claim is lodged.
