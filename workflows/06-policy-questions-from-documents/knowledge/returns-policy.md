# Returns policy (customer-facing, version 2, March 2026)

Questions about this policy: ask the Customer team lead.

## §1 Change of mind

Customers can return an unused item in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a refund or an
exchange.

## §2 Faulty items

If an item is faulty, the customer is entitled to a repair, a replacement or a refund under the Consumer
Guarantees Act. We ask for a photo of the fault and the order number. There is no fixed time limit: what is
reasonable depends on the product and the fault.

## §3 Refund method and shipping costs

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of us receiving the return. For change of
mind returns, the customer pays the return shipping and the original shipping is not refunded. For faulty items
we pay the return shipping.

## §4 What we can't accept

Items that have been used, engraved or personalised can't be returned for change of mind. Gift cards can't be
refunded.
