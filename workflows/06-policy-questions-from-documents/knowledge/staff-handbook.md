# Staff handbook (version 3, July 2026)

Questions about this handbook: ask the People & Culture lead.

## §1 Hours and breaks

Standard hours are 8:30 am to 5:00 pm, Monday to Friday. You have a paid 15-minute break in the morning and in
the afternoon, and an unpaid 30-minute lunch break.

## §2 Annual leave

Full-time staff receive 4 weeks of paid annual leave each year. Request leave in the HR system at least 2 weeks
before you want to take it. The warehouse limits leave in the 4 weeks before Christmas.

## §3 Expenses

You may spend up to $100 on a work expense without approval; keep the receipt and claim it within 30 days.
Anything over $100 needs your manager's approval before you buy it.

## §4 Working from home

Office staff may work from home up to 2 days a week when their manager agrees. Warehouse roles work on site.

## §5 Using AI tools at work

Use only the AI tools the company has approved: our company Claude workspace and our company Gemini
account. Never paste customer names, addresses, phone numbers, order details or payment details into any
other AI tool. Check every AI output before you send it or rely on it; you are responsible for what you send.
