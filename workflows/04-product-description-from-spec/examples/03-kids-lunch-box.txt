PRODUCT SPEC SHEET

Product: Kids' bento lunch box
SKU: TW-LBX-01
Colours: Coral, Ocean, Lemon

Design
- 4 compartments, 1.2 litres in total
- One-hand latch that children aged 5 and up can open
- Recommended for ages 3 and up

Materials
- Body and lid: polypropylene; seal: food-grade silicone
- BPA-free (tested by an accredited lab, report LAB-2026-03321)

Care and limits
- Base is dishwasher safe (top rack); hand wash the lid
- Not microwave safe
- Leak-resistant for thick foods such as yoghurt or sauces; thin liquids such as juice may leak

Warranty: 2 years on the latch
