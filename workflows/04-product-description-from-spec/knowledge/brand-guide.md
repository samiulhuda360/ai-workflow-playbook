# Brand guide (for product copy)

## Voice

- Warm, plain and practical. Write like a friend who has used the product for a month.
- Short sentences. Lead with what the product does for the customer, then the facts.
- New Zealand English spelling (colour, litre, organise).
- No exclamation marks. No hype.

## Words we use

durable, keeps drinks cold, keeps drinks hot, easy to clean, made to last, repairable, refillable, recycled
(only when the spec says recycled)

## Words and claims we never use

- Superlatives: best, world's best, #1, ultimate, perfect, revolutionary, game-changing.
- Health and safety claims: healthy, healthier, non-toxic, chemical-free, toxin-free, antibacterial,
  antimicrobial, hygienic, cures, prevents, boosts, detox, immune.
- Environmental claims we can't prove: eco-friendly, environmentally friendly, sustainable, green, planet-friendly,
  zero waste, plastic-free, carbon neutral.
- Guarantees: guaranteed, lifetime guarantee (we offer a 2-year warranty; mention it only if the spec does).

## Claims rules

- Every fact (size, material, temperature times, certificates, warranty) must come from the spec sheet.
- A test result or certificate is quoted exactly as the spec states it, with who tested it. For example:
  "BPA-free (tested by an accredited lab)". Never shorten it to "safe" or "non-toxic".
- If the spec says something is not dishwasher safe, say "hand wash only". Don't hide it.

## Formats

| Channel | Title | Body |
|---|---|---|
| Website | up to 70 characters | description up to 600 characters, plus up to 5 bullet points of up to 150 characters each |
| Marketplace | up to 200 characters, starting with the product type | up to 5 bullet points of up to 250 characters each |
