# Getting real work done with AI

60 minutes · one workflow you will actually use · the checks that keep it safe

---

## The task we'll fix today

A supplier email arrives. Someone reads it, updates the order tracker, writes a reply.

**Today:** time one before the session and put the number here. Easy to miss a changed date.
**With a workflow:** paste, check the flagged lines, edit the reply. Time it again at the end of the session.

---

## Chat vs workflow

| Chat | Workflow |
|---|---|
| A new prompt every time | The same instructions every time |
| Output in a different shape each time | A fixed format you can copy into a sheet |
| You hope it's right | Built-in checks tell you what to verify |
| Lives in one person's head | Shared, improved, measured |

---

## What AI is good at

- Pulling structured facts out of messy text (emails, notes, reviews)
- First drafts in a fixed voice
- Sorting and summarising large batches
- Answering from documents you give it, with citations

---

## Where it goes wrong

- It fills gaps with confident guesses: a date for "Monday", a reason nobody gave
- It drops the one serious complaint inside 200 happy reviews
- It answers from general knowledge when your policy is silent

**Each of these happened in our tests. Each workflow has a rule and a check for it.**

---

## Three rules in every workflow

1. **Only from the input.** Nothing invented: names, numbers, dates.
2. **Say when you don't know.** "Reason not given", "not covered in our documents".
3. **Flag what a person must decide.** Price changes, safety, legal, anything unclear.

---

## Demo: meeting notes → actions

- Paste notes → summary, decisions, actions, open questions
- Every action shows the exact sentence it came from
- An action nobody took becomes an open question, not a guess

---

## Your turn (20 minutes)

1. Everyone: meeting notes → actions, on notes from your own week
2. Choose one: reviews → themes, or a policy question with a citation
3. For each output: find **one thing you would check**, and check it

---

## The checks habit

Before you trust an output:

- Search for one quoted sentence. Is it really there?
- Pick one number. Is it in the source?
- Read everything flagged for a person yourself.

You are responsible for what you send (Staff handbook §5).

---

## Data you never paste

Customer names, addresses, phone numbers, order or payment details, staff records.

**Only in the approved company tools**, and only what the task needs.
