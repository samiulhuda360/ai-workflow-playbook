# Workshop exercises

Use the shared project your facilitator set up. If you use your own notes or emails, remove customer names,
addresses and order details first.

## Exercise 1 (everyone, 8 minutes): meeting notes → actions

1. Paste the notes from a meeting you had this week, with the date at the top. (No notes? Use
   [this example](../workflows/02-meeting-notes-to-actions/examples/03-no-owner.txt).)
2. Check one action: search the notes for its **source** sentence. Is it word for word?
3. Is there an action with no owner? Who should own it?

**Write down:** one thing you checked, and whether it was right.

## Exercise 2 (choose, 8 minutes): reviews → themes

1. Paste [these 20 lunch-box reviews](../workflows/03-customer-reviews-to-themes/examples/02-lunch-boxes.txt).
2. Read every review in **needs a person** in the original list. Would you have spotted them all?
3. Pick one theme and check two of its review IDs.

## Exercise 3 (choose, 8 minutes): a policy question with a citation

1. Ask two questions your customers or new staff ask you often.
2. Open the cited section. Does it say what the answer claims?
3. Ask one question the documents don't cover. Does it say so, and tell you who to ask?
